# Using Digital Marketing Channels to Drive the Buyer’s Journey

Effective digital marketing depends on understanding your audience, but your approach can vary depending on your industry, company size, and whether you’re selling ==business-to-business== (B2B) or ==business-to-consumer== (B2C).

**If you're selling B2B, your focus is getting quality leads online and connecting them with your sales team.** 
- Use websites and LinkedIn as your primary tools. 
- Your content should be tailored to business-related needs and challenges. 

**If you're selling B2C, you want to drive sales straight from your website, with less need for salespeople.** 
- This means highlighting product features early and speeding up the buying process. 
- Platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest are often more effective than business-focused ones. 


**Buyer’s journey:** 
The journey a customer takes before making a purchase.
![[buyer journey.png]]
This journey typically involves three stages:
● Awareness, where a prospect identifies a problem .
● Consideration, where they research potential solutions. 
● Decision, where they choose a specific solution.

**Customer journey mapping:**
- A process that visually outlines a customer’s complete experience with your brand.
- The experience across channels should feel [^2]cohesive.
- When individuals circulate your content, it fosters authenticity and credibility, which is crucial in today's digital world.
- Provide everything they need in order to choose you.

During ==the awareness stage==, ==consider using infographics and short-form videos==. Infographics are easily distributed. They boost your social media visibility when others pass along your content. Short-form videos also spread quickly, exposing your brand to fresh audiences. Depending on your industry, combining short videos with influencer marketing can expand your reach and build trust. When individuals circulate your content, it fosters authenticity and credibility, which is crucial in today's digital world. 

During ==the consideration stage==, consumers actively seek solutions. So, ==offer them resources like== ==eBooks, free samples, and webinars==. Since they’re more comprehensive than blog posts or infographics, eBooks effectively generate leads by prompting contact information exchange. Free samples reduce perceived risk, facilitating evaluation, while webinars provide in-depth, interactive engagement. For all these offers, collect contact information to fuel strategic email marketing. 

In ==the decision stage==, when buyers are ready to convert, make it easy for them. Provide everything they need to choose you. ==Use case studies, testimonials, and personalized offers to drive action.== Case studies demonstrate how your solution solves problems, combining detailed information with data. If case studies aren't ideal, leverage social proof with brief testimonials throughout your site, like customer photos with branded hashtags. Targeted email marketing can deliver personalized offers and clear calls-to-action.

### Creating your Digital Marketing Strategy:

#### To create a digital marketing strategy: 
1.  Build your buyer personas. 
2.  Identify your goals and needed digital marketing tools. 
3.  Evaluate your existing digital channels and assets. 
4.  Plan your digital marketing campaigns.

---
1. **Buyer personas :**
- Semi-fictional representations of your ideal customer(s) that can be created by researching, surveying, and interviewing your business's target audience.
- Outline their goals, [^3]pain points, typical day, information sources, and decision-making processes.

1. **SMART goal :**
- A goal that is specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.

1. **Analyze how your channels and assets are performing in terms of reach, engagement, and conversions.**

2. **Campaign planning involves outlining the specific activities, content, channels, and timelines needed to achieve your goals.**
